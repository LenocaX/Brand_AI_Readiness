# Engagement Audit Checks

## 1. Page Purpose

Check whether the page communicates a clear purpose through:

- title
- H1
- introductory content
- meaningful navigation labels

Do not require identical wording across these elements.

---

## 2. Primary Orientation

Check whether users can identify:

- what the page is about
- what the site offers
- where they can go next

A missing H1 alone is not sufficient evidence of poor engagement.

---

## 3. Navigation

Inspect:

- primary navigation
- internal links
- navigation labels
- home link

Navigation should help visitors understand the site's structure.

---

## 4. Calls to Action

Detect meaningful action-oriented controls and links.

Examples:

- Get started
- Contact us
- Buy now
- Request demo
- Sign up
- View pricing
- Learn more

Do not require a CTA on informational, legal, policy,
or other pages where an action is not contextually expected.

---

## 5. Context Retention

Check whether users can understand their current location
within the site using:

- descriptive headings
- page titles
- breadcrumbs
- navigation
- descriptive internal links

Avoid treating generic links such as "click here" as strong
contextual navigation.

---

## 6. Dead Ends

Identify pages with:

- no useful internal links
- no primary navigation
- no meaningful next step

A dead-end finding should consider page type and context.

---

## 7. Link Quality

Prefer descriptive internal links.

Examples of weak link text:

- click here
- read more
- here
- learn more

Generic links are not automatically a defect when surrounding
context makes their destination clear.

---

## 8. Content Hierarchy

Check:

- H1 presence
- heading structure
- logical heading progression
- excessive heading repetition

Do not require perfect heading numbering.

---

## 9. False Positive Control

Do not report:

- every short page as poor engagement
- every page without CTA as poor engagement
- every page without breadcrumbs as poor engagement
- every generic link as a critical issue
- every page with one internal link as a dead end

The finding must have evidence and context.

---

## 10. Recommended Improvements

The skill may recommend proactive improvements such as:

- clearer primary navigation
- stronger page-specific next steps
- descriptive internal linking
- clearer value proposition
- improved content hierarchy

Recommendations must be relevant to the observed page structure.
